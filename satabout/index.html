SatAbout

Privacy Policy · Effective October 28, 2026

SatAbout lets you sketch roundabouts and measure intersections on satellite imagery. The short version: the developer never receives any of your data, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What the app stores

  • Your sites: each site's name, notes, the map location you saved it at, and how the map was zoomed and turned, so it reopens where you left it.
  • Settings: your choice of feet or meters, stored on your device.

Your location

If you tap the "my location" button, the app asks for your location while you're using it, only to move the map to where you are. Your location is never stored, synced or sent anywhere by the app.

Maps and search

Satellite imagery, place search and street names come from Apple Maps. To show them, your device sends Apple the area of the map you're viewing and what you type in search, as any Apple Maps app does; Apple's handling of that is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer never sees it.

Where it is stored

  • On your iPhone and iPad.
  • In your own private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on, so your sites sync between your devices. Apple holds this data; the developer cannot see or access it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or servers run by the developer.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services.
  • No selling, sharing or using your data for marketing.

Your choices

  • Change or remove location access at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services ▸ SatAbout.
  • Rename or delete any site in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

Sketches are not designs

Measurements and drawings in SatAbout come from satellite imagery, not a survey. They are for exploring ideas and are not engineering designs.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Open an issue or send feedback through TestFlight.
