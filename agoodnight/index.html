A Good Night

Privacy Policy · Effective October 9, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026

A Good Night helps you see how late eating, alcohol and caffeine relate to your sleep. It is built so that your information stays with you. The short version: the developer never receives any of your data, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What the app uses

  • What you log: late food, drinks and caffeine, the times you had them, your morning sleep ratings and notes, how stressful the previous day was (1–5, if you choose to answer), and any experiments you run (such as "no food after 8 pm for two weeks").
  • Supplements and medications: you can log supplements or medications by name (no doses); they sync through your iCloud like the rest of your log and are never written to Apple Health.
  • Apple Health (with your permission): the app reads sleep analysis, heart rate variability and resting heart rate. It also reads your workouts and their effort scores to see how evening exercise lines up with your sleep; they stay on your device. It also reads your time in daylight and step count from Apple Health to look for patterns with your sleep. If you turn the options on in Settings, it also saves the caffeine and alcoholic drinks you log to Apple Health, and reads caffeine and drinks that other apps saved there.

Where it is stored

  • On your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch, iPad or Mac if you use the app there.
  • In your own private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on, so your log and your settings (such as reminder times, your bedtime goal and favorite drinks) sync between your devices and are restored on a new iPhone. Apple holds this data; the developer cannot see or access it.
  • Health data read from Apple Health is used on your device to build your sleep summaries and insights. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or servers run by the developer.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services.
  • No selling, sharing or using your data for marketing. Health data is never used for advertising.

Apple Watch, notifications, widgets and Siri

The Apple Watch app keeps its own copy of your log, synced through your iCloud like your other devices. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets, complications, controls and Siri shortcuts only show or record information on your devices.

iPad and Mac

On iPad and Mac, A Good Night only displays your data. It shows what you logged on your iPhone and Apple Watch, synced through your iCloud. On iPad it reads the same Apple Health data as on iPhone, from that device, and never writes to it. A Mac has no Apple Health, so nothing is read there.

Summaries

Morning recaps and weekly digests are written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Your data is not sent to Apple or anyone else to write them. On devices without Apple Intelligence the app shows the same points as a list.

Exporting

You can export your log as CSV files from Settings. The files go only where you choose to send them.

Your choices

  • Change or remove Health access at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Health.
  • Edit or delete any entry from the Tonight or History tabs.
  • Turn saving to or reading from Apple Health on or off in the app's Settings. Caffeine and drinks the app already saved stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

Insights are not medical advice

The app shows patterns in your own data. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
