A Good Night

Privacy Policy · Effective October 7, 2026

A Good Night helps you see how late eating, alcohol and caffeine relate to your sleep. It is built so that your information stays with you. The short version: the developer never receives any of your data, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

What the app uses

  • What you log: late food, drinks and caffeine, the times you had them, and your morning sleep ratings and notes.
  • Apple Health (read only, with your permission): sleep analysis, heart rate variability and resting heart rate. The app does not write anything to Apple Health.

Where it is stored

  • On your iPhone.
  • In your own private iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on, so your log syncs between your devices. Apple holds this data; the developer cannot see or access it.
  • Health data read from Apple Health is used on your device to build your sleep summaries and insights. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or servers run by the developer.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services.
  • No selling, sharing or using your data for marketing. Health data is never used for advertising.

Notifications, widgets and Siri

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Widgets, controls and Siri shortcuts only show or record information on your device.

Your choices

  • Change or remove Health access at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Health.
  • Edit or delete any entry from the Tonight or History tabs.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

Insights are not medical advice

The app shows patterns in your own data. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Open an issue or send feedback through TestFlight.
