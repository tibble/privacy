A Good Night

Support

A Good Night logs late food, drinks and caffeine and shows which of them go with worse sleep for you, using sleep data from Apple Health.

Questions or problems? Open an issue and it will be answered there. Issues are public, so please leave out health details you'd rather keep private.

Insights says "N more nights"

Comparisons need at least 7 nights with a habit and 7 clean nights (nothing late logged), all with sleep data. The fuller "Accounting for the others" view needs about 30 logged nights.

The Morning tab shows no sleep

The app reads sleep from Apple Health. Check that something records sleep there (an Apple Watch with Sleep tracking on, or another tracker), and that A Good Night may read Sleep in Settings ▸ Health ▸ Data Access & Devices. The morning rating works without it.

My watch doesn't show what I logged on my iPhone

iPhone and Apple Watch stay in step through your iCloud account, which can take a few minutes. Both need to be signed in to the same iCloud account.

How do I get my data out, or delete it?

Settings ▸ Export as CSV saves everything you logged and each night's numbers. Deleting an entry (swipe it on Tonight or in a night's detail) deletes it everywhere. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; to remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

Is this medical advice?

No. The app shows patterns in your own data. Talk to a doctor about ongoing sleep problems.
