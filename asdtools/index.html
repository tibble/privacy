ASD Tools

Privacy Policy · Effective October 7, 2026

ASD Tools is the account administration app used by Antietam School District's IT staff. It lets an authorized administrator look up and manage staff and student accounts in the district's own Microsoft and Google systems. The short version: the developer never receives any data from the app. The app talks directly to the district's own services, as the signed-in administrator, and nothing else.

Who can use it

Only district staff whom Antietam School District IT has assigned to the app can sign in, using their district Microsoft account. There are no accounts or sign-ups run by the developer.

What the app uses

  • District directory information: staff and student account details from the district's Microsoft Entra ID / Microsoft 365 tenant (via Microsoft Graph) and Google Workspace (via the Google Admin SDK). Examples are names, email addresses, account status, groups, licenses, sign-in activity and registered sign-in methods. The app reads these, and changes them, only with the signed-in administrator's own permissions, and only when the administrator asks.
  • Student password sheets: when an administrator resets a student password, the app may record it on the district's own Google Sheet for that school.
  • Door access (optional): if an administrator enters a Verkada API key, the app reads door access events, profile photos and door camera stills from the district's Verkada system. This is read-only.
  • Roster files (optional): a roster CSV the administrator chooses to open is read on the device to compare against the directory.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used to unlock the app. Apple handles this. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never sees biometric data.

Where it is stored

  • Sign-in tokens and the Verkada API key are kept in the device keychain.
  • A list of account names and email addresses is saved on the device so name suggestions appear quickly. It is protected by the device's encryption and deleted when you sign out.
  • An audit log of the actions taken in the app (who, what, which account, result) is kept on the device. It never contains passwords or access passes. It leaves the device only if the administrator shares it.
  • Report settings such as filters and thresholds are saved on the device.
  • Exported reports are saved only where the administrator chooses to save them.
  • Everything else, including the directory itself, stays in the district's own Microsoft, Google and Verkada systems and is governed by the district's agreements with those providers.

What the app does not do

  • No servers run by the developer. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing or use of any data for marketing.
  • Passwords and Temporary Access Passes are shown once, hidden from screen recordings, and never written to the audit log. When copied, they stay on this device and the clipboard clears itself.

Student records

Student information the app displays is education-record data that belongs to Antietam School District. The app is a tool for district staff to do their jobs, and it doesn't collect or keep that data for any other purpose. Questions about student records themselves should go to the district.

Your choices

  • Sign out from the account menu. Signing out removes the sign-in tokens and the cached name list from the device.
  • Remove the Verkada API key at any time from the door access settings in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device, including the audit log.
  • District IT can revoke anyone's access by removing their assignment to the app in Microsoft Entra ID.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Open an issue or send feedback through TestFlight.
